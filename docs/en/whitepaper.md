# Whitepaper

**Status: proposal · v0.1 · September 24, 2026**

This document is an initial proposal for discussion, not an announcement of an established organization or confirmed events.

## Purpose

ZK Fukuoka is a planned community based in Fukuoka for developing engineers who understand and build with zero-knowledge proofs. We connect mathematical understanding with implementation, peer review, and clear communication.

## What we value

- Curiosity over assumed expertise: make room for questions and learning from mistakes.
- Learn by building: combine reading, hands-on exercises, and small projects.
- Open knowledge: maintain materials and decision records in GitHub, in Japanese and English.
- Honest scope: distinguish educational prototypes from production-ready systems.

## Proposed activities

Use the [syllabus](./learn/) as the common learning path. Plan study sessions, implementation workshops, and project demonstrations, with retrospectives informing the next cycle. Frequency, venues, instructors, and remote participation are to be decided.

## Community operations

Start with maintainers and contributors reviewing improvements through Issues and pull requests. Material decisions should capture context, alternatives, and consequences in [ADRs](./adr/). Roles, decision authority, and operating rules remain proposals for future agreement.

## Learning outcomes

Help participants explain proof properties, reproduce examples, write small circuits, and discuss limitations. Consider feedback such as reproducibility, learning obstacles, and peer review quality; no numerical targets are committed yet.

## Next decisions

Agree on the first cohort, scope, schedule, tools, conduct guidelines, and licensing. Participation fees, funding, partnerships, and certification have not been established.
