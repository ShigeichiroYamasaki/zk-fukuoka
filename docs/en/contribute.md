# Get involved

ZK Fukuoka is in formation. Event dates, venues, and registration are not yet announced. For now, help shape the materials and community through GitHub.

## Ways to help

- **Ask a question:** open an Issue describing what was unclear and what you tried.
- **Improve a lesson:** propose a correction or a reproducible example.
- **Translate:** update the Japanese and English pages together where possible.
- **Discuss the design:** suggest changes to the whitepaper or an ADR.

[Open an Issue](https://github.com/ShigeichiroYamasaki/zk-fukuoka/issues/new) · [Browse the repository](https://github.com/ShigeichiroYamasaki/zk-fukuoka)

## A small contribution

1. Find a page through the [syllabus](./learn/).
2. Open an Issue to discuss a larger change, or create a branch for a small correction.
3. Edit Markdown under `docs/` and its counterpart under `docs/en/`.
4. Build locally, then open a pull request describing the change and checks performed.

## Working together

Respect different backgrounds, welcome questions, and offer constructive feedback. Use dummy data in examples; keep personal information and secrets out of public Issues. A full code of conduct and moderation process are still to be agreed.
