# ADR-0003 · Motivation-first, three-act syllabus

- Date: 2026-09-24
- Status: proposed
- Replaces: [ADR-0002](./0002-learning)
- Source: Shigeichiro Yamasaki, syllabus proposal dated 2026/09/07

## Context

Starting with mathematical tools can obscure why learners need them. The new proposal establishes goals and motivations before introducing tools, then returns to those goals through actual protocols.

## Proposal

Use the [three-act, 15-session syllabus](../learn/) as the learning entry point:

1. Goals and motivations (Sessions 1–2).
2. Tools: mathematics, information theory, and cryptography (Sessions 3–10).
3. Integration: Groth16, PLONK, STARK, and further directions (Sessions 11–15).

Explicitly connect Acts I and III so learners retain a sense of purpose during Act II. Publish the Japanese proposal and an aligned English translation. Existing finite-field exercises become supplementary material for Session 3.

## Alternative

The previous four-stage draft began with foundations. Retain it in ADR-0002 as history; the current site follows this three-act proposal.

## Consequences and review

Update navigation and learning materials around the 15 sessions. Session materials, exercises, delivery details, and prerequisite support still need development. This is a curriculum proposal, not a confirmed event schedule or community-ratified operating decision.
